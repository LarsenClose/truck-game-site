Privacy Policy for Truck: Dig & Drive

Last updated: August 10, 2025

Overview

Truck: Dig & Drive ("we", "our", or "the game") respects your privacy. This policy explains our data practices.

Information We Collect

Anonymous Game Data: We collect anonymous gameplay statistics (high scores, game mode preferences) solely to improve the game experience. This data is associated with a random device ID, not your personal identity.

Information We Don't Collect

We do NOT collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Contact information
  • Photos or media
  • Financial information

How We Use Information

Anonymous gameplay data is used only to:

  • Display global leaderboards
  • Sync your progress across devices
  • Improve game balance and features

Data Storage

Game data is stored securely using Google Firebase. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Children's Privacy

Our game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase for data storage and leaderboards. View their privacy policy at firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Your Rights

You can reset your game data at any time through the game settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted at this URL.
